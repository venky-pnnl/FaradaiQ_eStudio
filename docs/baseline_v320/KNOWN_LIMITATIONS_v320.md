# FaradaIQ Studio v320 known limitations

- Dense potential-dependent datasets are summarized in PPT overlays using deterministic representative potentials plus low-emphasis background curves. The Browser remains the complete interactive view of every spectrum.
- DRT potential maps are descriptive. Mechanism assignment still requires independent electrochemical/chemical evidence.
- Quantum-capacitance, flat-band, carrier-density and PZC interpretations remain conditional on the assumptions and validity checks documented by the analysis.
