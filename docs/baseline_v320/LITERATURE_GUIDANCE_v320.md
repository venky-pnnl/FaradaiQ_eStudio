# FaradaIQ Studio v320 literature guidance

v320 does not change the scientific interpretation rules introduced in v315-v318. Standard EIS/DRT overlays are presentation views. Equivalent-circuit, DRT, capacitance, PZC, Mott-Schottky and quantum-capacitance interpretation must continue to respect model identifiability, frequency dependence, series capacitance, and mechanism-assignment limits.
